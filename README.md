🧠 What SignalCart Does
Most small business websites don’t fail because of traffic—they fail because of friction.
SignalCart identifies and fixes that friction by analyzing:
Headline clarity & value proposition
Call-to-action strength and placement
Page structure and user flow
Trust signals (reviews, guarantees, etc.)
UX and conversion blockers
It then provides:
Clear explanations of what’s wrong
Why it’s hurting conversions
Specific, ready-to-use fixes (including rewritten copy)
