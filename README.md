# 🚀 SignalCart

> Paste a URL. Get a conversion audit. Increase your sales.

SignalCart is an AI-powered conversion optimization tool that analyzes websites and product pages to identify why visitors aren’t converting—and delivers instant, ready-to-use improvements.

---

## 🌐 Live Demo  
https://signalcart.zite.so  

Try it by entering any website URL (like a Shopify product page or local business site).

---

## 🧠 What It Does  

SignalCart identifies what’s hurting conversions:
- Unclear messaging  
- Weak calls-to-action  
- Poor structure  
- Missing trust signals  

Then gives:
- What’s wrong  
- Why it matters  
- Exact fixes (rewritten copy)

---

## ⚡ Features  
- Conversion audit  
- Before vs after copy  
- Friction detection  
- Fast results
